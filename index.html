Rooted in Kerala's Spice Hills

Founded by K.P. Mathai & Sons as a humble hill produce dealer in Rajakumari, our journey began with a simple vision: to share the authentic, unadulterated flavors of the Western Ghats. Over generations, we have forged enduring relationships with local estate families, ensuring that every spice we source carries the rich, aromatic legacy of Kerala directly to your kitchen.

1976: The Beginning

Our story starts in the lush terrains of Kerala. By trading directly out of the hills, we built deep, ethical relationships with generational estate farmers—bonds that remain the unshakable foundation of our sourcing today.

2015: Vava Spices (P) Ltd

The family legacy evolved into a modern private limited company. While maintaining our traditional roots, we introduced state-of-the-art grading halls and rigorous quality control labs to guarantee global premium standards.

Today: Global Exporters

Vava Spices stands as a premier global exporter. From curated household packs to massive international bulk contracts, we ensure every single lot of spices is fully traceable and exported safely worldwide.
