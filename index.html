Rooted in Kerala's Spice Hills

Founded in 1976 by K.P. Mathai & Sons under the name K P Mathai hill produces dealer, our family has spent generations trading the finest produce of the Western Ghats.

Our Journey

1976: The Beginning

K.P. Mathai & Sons begin trading out of the Kerala hills, building deep relationships with estate families that still supply us today.

2015: Vava Spices (P) Ltd

The legacy is rebuilt as a private limited company. We maintain our original sourcing, with new grading halls and lab checks.

Today: Hills to Kitchens

From household packs to bulk contracts across India, we ensure every lot of spices is fully traceable back to the estate.
